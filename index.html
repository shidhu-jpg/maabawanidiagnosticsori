© 2026 Maa Bhawani Diagnostics · Areraj, Bihar

Maa Bhawani Diagnostics has been serving patients in Areraj and the surrounding villages for over 35 years — since 1989. What began as a small local pathology centre has grown into a fully-equipped diagnostic lab, while staying true to its promise: reliable reports, honest prices, and a team that treats every patient like family.

We offer pathology (blood, urine, stool) testing, digital X-Ray, dental OPG, and home sample collection, backed by ISO-certified processes and an Udyam-registered establishment.
